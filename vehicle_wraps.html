Vehicle Wrap FAQ

Before you wrap your vehicle.

Common questions about vehicle wraps, installation, finishes and care.

What is the difference between a full wrap and a partial wrap?
A full wrap changes the appearance of most or all wrapped exterior body panels. A partial wrap focuses on selected areas such as the roof, hood, trim or other accents.
What colors and finishes are available?
Available options vary by manufacturer and include gloss, satin, matte, metallic, glitter, color-shifting and specialty finishes. We can help narrow down options based on the look you want for your vehicle.
How long does a vehicle wrap last?
Wrap longevity varies based on the material, installation, vehicle usage, sun exposure, storage and maintenance. We can discuss expected performance and care requirements for the specific film selected for your project.
Will a vehicle wrap damage my paint?
No. When professionally installed and removed, vinyl wraps actually protect your original paint and can be removed without causing any damage.
Can you wrap individual parts instead of the entire vehicle?
Yes. Depending on the project, roofs, hoods, trim and other exterior areas can be wrapped independently to create contrast or accents without completing a full color change.
How should I care for a wrapped vehicle?
Proper hand washing and vinyl-safe products are recommended. Care requirements can vary depending on the film and finish selected for your vehicle.
How do I get a quote?
Contact POLISH LAB with the year, make and model of your vehicle along with the color or finish you have in mind. Inspiration photos are also helpful when planning your project.
