About PPF

Protection you barely see.

Paint Protection Film, commonly known as PPF, is a durable urethane film applied over painted exterior surfaces to create a sacrificial layer between your paint and the road.

Designed for high-impact areas or complete vehicle coverage, PPF helps reduce damage from rock chips, road debris, bug impacts and minor surface abrasions while allowing the original finish to remain visible underneath.

Paint Protection Film FAQ

Before you protect your vehicle.

Common questions about PPF, coverage, installation and maintenance.

What does paint protection film protect against?
PPF is designed to help protect painted surfaces against rock chips, road debris, bug impacts, light abrasions and other everyday exposure.

It is a sacrificial protective layer and does not make the vehicle or paint completely damage-proof.
Will PPF change the appearance of my vehicle?
Clear PPF is designed to be visually subtle and allow the original paint color and finish to remain visible.

Final appearance can vary depending on the film selected, the condition of the paint and the type of finish being protected.
Does PPF self-heal?
Many premium PPF products use self-healing top-coat technology designed to reduce the appearance of light surface marks when exposed to heat.

The exact characteristics depend on the specific film selected for your vehicle.
How long does PPF last?
PPF longevity depends on the specific film, vehicle usage, environmental exposure, storage and maintenance.

During your consultation, we can discuss the expected performance and manufacturer coverage for the film selected for your project.
Do I need full-body PPF?
Not necessarily. The right coverage depends on your vehicle, driving habits and protection goals.

Some owners prioritize high-impact front-end areas, while others choose extended or full-body coverage for more comprehensive protection.
Can PPF be installed over ceramic coating?
PPF installation requires properly prepared surfaces. If the vehicle already has a ceramic coating, the existing surface will need to be evaluated and appropriately prepared before film is installed.

If you are planning both services, PPF is generally installed first and ceramic coating can then be considered for the finished protection package.
Can ceramic coating be applied over PPF?
Compatible ceramic coatings can be applied over many paint protection films to add hydrophobic behavior and make routine maintenance easier.

Product compatibility should be confirmed based on the specific PPF and coating being used.
How do I get a PPF quote?
Contact POLISH LAB with your vehicle's year, make and model along with the level of coverage you're considering.

We can help you determine whether front-end, extended or full-body protection makes the most sense for your vehicle.
