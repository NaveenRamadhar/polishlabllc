Paint Correction

Bring the paint back to life.

Even properly maintained vehicles can develop swirls, haze and surface imperfections over time. Paint correction uses controlled machine polishing to refine the clear coat and improve the overall appearance of the finish.

01

Swirl Reduction

Reduce the appearance of wash-induced swirls and other correctable defects that become visible under direct lighting.

02

Improved Clarity

Refine hazy or dull paint to improve reflection, clarity and overall surface appearance.

03

Depth & Gloss

Proper polishing can restore depth and gloss that may have been hidden beneath years of light surface defects.

04

Coating Preparation

Refining the paint before ceramic coating allows the finish to look its best before long-term protection is applied.

Correction Options

Every paint finish is different.

The appropriate level of correction depends on the condition of the paint, the defects present and the result you're looking to achieve.

01

Paint Enhancement

A lighter polishing process focused on improving gloss and reducing minor surface defects for an overall cleaner finish.

02

Single-Stage Correction

A more focused polishing process designed to improve clarity and reduce a greater amount of visible swirls and correctable defects.

03

Multi-Stage Correction

A more intensive correction process for paint requiring additional defect removal and refinement before the final finish is achieved.

Ceramic Coating

Protect the finish you've created.

Ceramic coating forms a durable protective layer over compatible exterior surfaces, helping improve water behavior, resist environmental contamination and make routine maintenance easier.

01

Hydrophobic Performance

Ceramic coatings create strong water behavior that helps water bead and move across protected surfaces more easily.

02

Enhanced Finish

On compatible gloss surfaces, ceramic coating can enhance depth, gloss and the overall presentation of the finish.

03

Easier Maintenance

Protected surfaces are generally easier to clean and maintain when paired with proper washing and ongoing vehicle care.

04

Environmental Protection

Adds a sacrificial layer that helps resist environmental contamination and exposure encountered during normal use.

Paint Correction & Ceramic Coating FAQ

Before we refine your finish.

Common questions about paint correction, ceramic coating and protecting painted, wrapped and PPF-covered vehicles.

What is paint correction?
Paint correction is a machine polishing process used to refine the vehicle's clear coat and reduce the appearance of correctable defects such as swirls, haze, oxidation and light surface imperfections.

The amount of correction possible depends on the condition and thickness of the paint, the type and depth of the defects and the appropriate approach for the vehicle.
Does paint correction remove every scratch?
No. Paint correction can significantly improve many surface defects, but deeper scratches or defects that extend beyond a safely correctable portion of the clear coat may remain.

We prioritize improving the finish while preserving the integrity of the paint.
Do I need paint correction before ceramic coating?
Not every vehicle requires the same level of correction. The paint is inspected first to determine whether polishing or correction is appropriate before coating.

Because ceramic coating does not remove paint defects, refining the finish beforehand can improve the final appearance when correction is needed.
What does ceramic coating do?
Ceramic coating creates a protective layer over compatible surfaces that can improve hydrophobic behavior, enhance the appearance of the finish and make routine maintenance easier.

It also provides resistance to environmental contamination, but it does not make the vehicle immune to scratches, rock chips or physical damage.
Can you ceramic coat a wrapped vehicle?
Yes. Compatible ceramic coatings can be applied to many vinyl wraps to help improve surface behavior and make routine maintenance easier.

The coating and preparation process should be selected based on the specific vinyl and whether the finish is gloss, satin, matte or specialty film.
Do you paint correct a vehicle before wrapping it?
Paint correction and vinyl wrapping serve different purposes. Before wrapping, the underlying paint is inspected and prepared for installation.

If the vehicle has paint defects that require additional attention before wrapping, the appropriate preparation can be discussed during the project inspection.
Can ceramic coating be applied over PPF?
Yes, compatible ceramic coatings can be applied over many paint protection films to improve hydrophobic behavior and make maintenance easier.

Compatibility depends on the specific film and coating being used.
Is ceramic coating the same as PPF?
No. Ceramic coating and PPF provide different types of protection.

Ceramic coating primarily improves surface behavior, contamination resistance and maintenance, while PPF provides a physical film layer designed to help protect against rock chips, road debris and minor abrasions.
How long does ceramic coating last?
Coating longevity depends on the product selected, surface preparation, vehicle usage, environmental exposure, storage and maintenance.

We can discuss the expected performance and manufacturer guidance for the specific coating selected for your vehicle.
How do I get a paint correction or ceramic coating quote?
Contact POLISH LAB with your vehicle's year, make and model and let us know whether the vehicle is painted, wrapped or protected with PPF.

Photos of the vehicle and its current finish are helpful when evaluating the project, although an in-person inspection may be recommended for paint correction.
