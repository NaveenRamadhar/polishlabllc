About POLISH LAB

Built Different. Finished Properly.

POLISH LAB is an Orlando automotive customization studio built around one idea: every modification should feel intentional. From styling and protection to performance and vehicle care, we bring every element together to create a finished vehicle that feels complete.

Our Story Start Your Build
Our Story

It started with the details.

POLISH LAB began with premium vehicle care and a simple obsession with the details that separate an ordinary result from an exceptional one.

That same approach now extends beyond detailing. Today, POLISH LAB brings together vehicle wraps, paint protection film, window tint, wheels, suspension, aero, lighting, interior upgrades, ceramic coating and other automotive customization services.

The vision is bigger than offering individual services. It's about creating complete vehicles where the color, stance, protection, components and finishing details all work together.

Our Philosophy
