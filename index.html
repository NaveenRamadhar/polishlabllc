FAQ

Before we start your build.

Answers to common questions about automotive customization, protection and vehicle care at POLISH LAB in Orlando.

What services does POLISH LAB offer?
POLISH LAB specializes in automotive styling, protection and customization including vehicle wraps, paint protection film (PPF), window tint, paint correction, ceramic coating, wheels and tires, suspension, body kits and aero, exhaust systems, starlight headliners, brake caliper color changes and premium detailing.

Services can be completed individually or combined into a more comprehensive build.
Can you help me plan a complete build?
Yes. You don't need to have every detail figured out before contacting us. Tell us what you drive, the look you're going for and the modifications you're considering.

We can help you plan the project so the color, protection, stance, wheels, aero and finishing details work together as one complete build.
Can I have multiple services completed at the same time?
Yes. Many POLISH LAB projects combine multiple services such as a vehicle wrap, window tint, wheels, suspension, aero or other customization.

Combining services also allows us to plan the vehicle's overall appearance before work begins.
Do you work on luxury, exotic and performance vehicles?
Yes. POLISH LAB works with luxury, exotic, performance and enthusiast-owned vehicles, as well as daily drivers being transformed or protected.

Every project is approached around the specific vehicle, materials, components and scope of work involved.
Can I bring my own parts for installation?
Customer-supplied parts may be installed depending on the vehicle, component and scope of work.

Contact us with your vehicle information and the specific part before your appointment so we can confirm compatibility and installation requirements.
How long will my project take?
Project timelines depend on the vehicle, services selected, material availability and complexity of the build.

Smaller services may be completed quickly, while full wraps, protection packages or multi-service transformations may require the vehicle for several days. An estimated timeline will be provided when your project is planned.
How do I get a quote?
Contact POLISH LAB with your vehicle's year, make and model along with the services you're interested in.

Photos of the vehicle and inspiration photos are helpful for wraps, wheels, aero, interior customization and more involved builds.
Do you offer mobile detailing?
Yes. POLISH LAB offers premium mobile detailing throughout Orlando and surrounding Central Florida communities.

Detailing appointments can be booked separately, while customization projects are handled by consultation or inquiry.
