Customization FAQ

Before we start your build.

Common questions about automotive customization and project planning at POLISH LAB.

What customization services do you offer?
POLISH LAB offers a growing range of automotive customization services including wheels and tires, suspension, body kits and aero, exhaust systems, brake caliper color changes, wheel powder coating, starlight headliners, ambient lighting and selected interior upgrades.

We also offer vehicle wraps, PPF, window tint, ceramic coating, paint correction and premium detailing.
Can you help me plan a complete build?
Yes. Tell us what you drive, the look you're going for and any modifications you're considering.

We can help organize the project so the color, wheels, stance, aero, lighting and finishing details work together rather than feeling like unrelated modifications.
Can I have multiple services completed at the same time?
Yes. Many projects combine multiple services such as a vehicle wrap, window tint, wheels, suspension, aero, caliper color changes or interior upgrades.

The scope and timeline will depend on the specific vehicle, components and services selected.
Can I bring my own parts?
Customer-supplied parts may be installed depending on the vehicle, component and project.

Contact us with your vehicle information and the exact part before your appointment so compatibility and installation requirements can be reviewed.
Can you source parts for my build?
Depending on the project, we can help identify and source components that fit the direction of your build.

Availability, pricing and lead times vary by vehicle, manufacturer and component.
How long does a customization project take?
Timelines vary depending on the vehicle, services selected, component availability and complexity of the project.

A single modification may require significantly less time than a complete multi-service transformation. An estimated timeline can be discussed once the scope of the build is established.
Do you work on luxury, exotic and performance vehicles?
Yes. POLISH LAB works with luxury, exotic, performance and enthusiast-owned vehicles as well as daily drivers being personalized, protected or transformed.
How do I get a quote?
Contact POLISH LAB with your vehicle's year, make and model along with the services or modifications you're considering.

Vehicle photos, links to parts and inspiration photos are helpful when planning more involved customization projects.
